Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

A. General

In this policy, the words 'we', 'our', and 'us' refer to Vansh Khari Bespoke Real Estate Advisory (operating through the domain www.vanshkhari.com) and the privacy mechanism outlined herein identifies personally identifiable or personal information that may be collected, how such information is handled, secured, and processed, and the choices you have regarding our use of this information.

Our practice provides bespoke, high-touch advisory counsel specializing in luxury residential acquisitions, independent estate evaluations, and strategic land investments across Delhi NCR and high-growth national corridors. Fiduciary discretion, personal privacy, and non-disclosure are foundational principles of our client engagements. We adhere strictly to the highest professional standards and statutory mandates under Indian jurisprudence.

B. Applicability

This privacy policy applies to all Users, patrons, investors, prospective buyers, landowners, and individuals who access the Website, submit contact requests, request research dossiers, or engage in private consultations with Vansh Khari. Users are required to read, comprehend, and accept the privacy policy before submitting any Personal Information.

If you have inadvertently submitted any personal information to Vansh Khari prior to reading the privacy statements set out herein, and you do not agree with the manner in which such information is collected, stored, or utilized, you may request access, modification, or complete deletion of all records held concerning you by writing directly to our advisory desk at contact@vanshkhari.com. Vansh Khari maintains confidential personal data of individual clients, and every user is entitled to require Vansh Khari to delete and securely destroy all such information (subject to necessary statutory record-keeping requirements).

This privacy policy applies exclusively to the Website and advisory services owned and operated by Vansh Khari. Vansh Khari does not exercise control over external platforms displayed as reference links, government land registry portals, municipal bodies, or third-party websites. Such third-party portals may place their own cookies, collect independent data, or solicit personal details, for which Vansh Khari is not responsible or liable. Accordingly, Vansh Khari does not make any representations concerning the privacy practices or policies of external websites. Users are strongly encouraged to inspect the individual privacy policies of any third-party resource they navigate to.

C. Personal & Advisory Information Collected

We collect only information that is strictly relevant and necessary to facilitate meaningful, high-level real estate advisory counsel. The categories of information collected include:

  • Direct Client Contact Identifiers: Full legal name, telephone / mobile number, direct WhatsApp communication handle, corporate or personal email address, and preferred city of communication.
  • Acquisition & Portfolio Criteria: Asset classification interests (e.g., ultra-luxury high-rise residences, penthouses, independent bungalows, farm villas, agricultural land parcels, or strategic arterial corridor tracts), estimated acquisition budget, intended deployment horizon, lifestyle or spatial specifications, and geographic focus within Delhi NCR (including Golf Course Road, DLF Phase 1-5, Southern Peripheral Road, New Gurgaon, Chhatarpur, Lutyens' periphery, or emerging growth belts).
  • Property & Title Verification Records: Where a client explicitly commissions bespoke due diligence on a specific land parcel or estate, voluntary copies of revenue documentation, mutation entries, Jamabandi records, registered sale deeds, layout approvals, or encumbrance verification documents provided to us for private analysis.
  • Technical & Session Metadata: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information including Internet Protocol (IP) address, operating system, browser variant, timestamp of consultation requests, and interaction metrics with educational articles published on the portal.

D. Lawful Basis & Purpose of Data Processing (DPDP Act, 2023)

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 read with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, all processing of personal data is carried out strictly upon voluntary consent and legitimate advisory purposes.

The collected information is utilized strictly for the following objectives:

  1. Facilitating direct, confidential 1-on-1 advisory consultations between the client and Vansh Khari.
  2. Synthesizing personalized research briefings, location suitability analyses, and valuation benchmarks.
  3. Coordinating independent legal title scrutiny, municipal master plan zoning reviews, and encumbrance checks with empaneled legal professionals upon client authorization.
  4. Responding to direct client queries received via our contact desk, email portal, or direct WhatsApp advisory channel.
  5. Maintaining professional records of advisory mandates and adhering to statutory legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution compliance under Indian law.

E. Absolute Non-Disclosure & Zero-Broker Sharing Guarantee

Uncompromising Discretion: Vansh Khari maintains a strict, unconditional Zero-Broker Sharing and Zero-Spam Policy. Your personal contact details, acquisition budgets, and transactional discussions are NEVER sold, rented, monetized, leased, traded, or distributed to third-party commercial brokerage networks, lead-generation aggregators, call centers, or telemarketing agencies.

Unlike open real estate listing platforms that broadcast client phone numbers to external third-party brokers, our practice operates with absolute fiduciary separation. When you communicate with Vansh Khari, your details remain exclusively within our private advisory office.

F. High-Value Due Diligence & Confidential Documentation

During strategic land acquisitions, farmhouse evaluations, or institutional asset reviews, clients frequently share sensitive property papers, revenue maps (Aks Shajra), and ownership titles. All such documents are treated with the highest degree of professional confidentiality.

Such records are reviewed solely for the purpose of identifying encumbrances, zoning restrictions, road-widening reservations, litigation history, or ownership ambiguities. They are disclosed to external certified legal practitioners or licensed surveyors solely with the client's prior explicit instruction and under binding mutual non-disclosure covenants.

G. Data Retention, Archival & Secure Disposal

Personal data collected for advisory consultations is retained only for the duration necessary to fulfill the specific advisory engagement or as mandated under applicable Indian taxation, contract, and statutory preservation laws (including the Indian Limitation Act, 1963).

Upon completion of an advisory mandate or upon formal written instruction from the client for data erasure, all digital files, notes, and correspondence will be securely wiped or permanently anonymized from our active advisory systems in accordance with standard data sanitization protocols.

H. Digital Analytics & Cookie Policy

The Website uses minimal, privacy-centric session cookies to ensure standard web performance, load balancing, and responsive layout delivery. These cookies do not store personally identifiable financial data or track users across external third-party websites.

Users have the liberty to disable or configure cookie acceptance through their individual browser settings (e.g., Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge). Disabling basic session cookies will not impair your ability to read articles or submit consultation requests on this website.

I. User Rights Under Indian Data Protection Laws

Every client and user retains comprehensive rights regarding their personal data under the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • Right to Access: You may request a formal summary of personal data held about you by our advisory practice.
  • Right to Correction & Updation: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate, misleading, or outdated personal information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the irrevocable deletion of your personal records from our databases, provided continued retention is not mandated by statutory law.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for future communication at any time by contacting our advisory desk.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate an authorized representative in accordance with provisions of the DPDP Act in the event of incapacity.

J. Information Security & Encryption Protocols

We deploy robust technical, physical, and managerial safeguards to protect client records against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Transmission of consultation data across www.vanshkhari.com is protected via modern Secure Socket Layer (SSL) and Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) 256-bit encryption.

Access to client files is restricted strictly to Vansh Khari and authorized advisory personnel bound by strict non-disclosure obligations. However, while we employ industry-standard security safeguards, no digital transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed as 100% immune from unlawful interception; users acknowledge this inherent digital reality when transmitting information online.

Any modifications will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. We encourage users to periodically review this page to stay informed about our data safeguarding practices. Continued use of the Website after any revision constitutes formal acknowledgement and acceptance of the updated terms.
