4. Independent Due Diligence & Legal Verification Mandate

  • RERA Compliance: All residential and commercial developments under review must be verified against their respective State Real Estate Regulatory Authority portals (e.g., HARERA Gurugram/Panchkula, UP-RERA, Delhi RERA).
  • Land Revenue Records: For agricultural, farmhouse, or strategic land parcels, verification of revenue registers (Jamabandi, Mutation / Dakhil Kharij, Khasra Girdawari, Aks Shajra maps) and 30-year non-encumbrance certificates must be authenticated at the competent Sub-Registrar / Tehsil office.
  • Physical Demarcation: Clients are advised to commission formal total-station survey boundary demarcation to confirm on-ground acreage and road setback reservations.
