Case Studies & Decision Frameworks

Our Work

We demonstrate how we think, evaluate, and conclude. Instead of displaying property prices and “buy now” buttons, we share our analytical frameworks.

Residential Case Evaluation • Delhi NCR Independent Villa Acquisition

Evaluating Spatial Orientation and Structural Build in a Multi-Crore Villa

The Objective: A private family client requested an evaluation of a prominent designer villa offered at a premium price.

What We Examined

Sun path analysis, basement waterproofing integrity, structural layout modifications, and acoustic buffer against neighborhood transit.

Risks Identified

Severe natural light deprivation in key daytime living areas and questionable structural modifications by prior owners.

Advisory Conclusion

Advised the client against acquisition despite the prestigious address, redirecting capital to a structurally superior asset.

Land & Corridor Evaluation • Strategic Expansion Belt Multi-Acre Agricultural Parcel

Verifying 30-Year Revenue Records & Access Rights for an Agricultural Land Parcel

The Objective: An investor sought long-term capital appreciation in a peri-urban agricultural corridor touted for upcoming arterial highway connectivity.

What We Examined

Complete 30-year Khasra/Khatauni trail, physical on-ground demarcation, and right-of-way access easement legality.

Risks Identified

Undisclosed coparcenary claims from a historical partition and lack of a legally registered ingress/egress road.

Advisory Conclusion

Coordinated legal clearance and renegotiated valuation to reflect easement encumbrances, saving the investor significant capital.
